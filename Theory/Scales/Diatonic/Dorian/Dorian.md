# Dorian

**Formula:** 1 2 ♭3 4 5 6 ♭7  
**Mode 2** of the major scale

Minor with a raised 6th. Soulful and a little hopeful. Think "Oye Como Va", "So What", lots of funk and blues-rock soloing.
