# 🎸 Scales

Every note has the scale in all 12 keys, with whole-neck maps (notes and intervals) and movable position shapes.

## Pentatonic
- [[Major Pentatonic]] — 1 2 3 5 6
- [[Minor Pentatonic]] — 1 ♭3 4 5 ♭7

## Diatonic (the 7 modes of the major scale)
| # | Mode | Formula | Feel |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [[Ionian (Major)]] | 1 2 3 4 5 6 7 | The major scale |
| 2 | [[Dorian]] | 1 2 ♭3 4 5 6 ♭7 | Minor with a raised 6th |
| 3 | [[Phrygian]] | 1 ♭2 ♭3 4 5 ♭6 ♭7 | Minor with a flat 2nd |
| 4 | [[Lydian]] | 1 2 3 ♯4 5 6 7 | Major with a raised 4th |
| 5 | [[Mixolydian]] | 1 2 3 4 5 6 ♭7 | Major with a flat 7th |
| 6 | [[Aeolian (Natural Minor)]] | 1 2 ♭3 4 5 ♭6 ♭7 | The natural minor scale |
| 7 | [[Locrian]] | 1 ♭2 ♭3 4 ♭5 ♭6 ♭7 | Flat 2nd and flat 5th |

> [!tip] Quick picks
> - Major-key song, easy solo → **Major Pentatonic** of the key
> - Minor key, rock/blues → **Minor Pentatonic**
> - Over a dominant 7 / bluesy major → **Mixolydian**
> - Minor with a brighter, funky feel → **Dorian**
