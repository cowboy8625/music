# Minor Pentatonic

**Formula:** 1 ♭3 4 5 ♭7

The natural minor scale minus the 2nd and 6th. The rock and blues workhorse. Add a ♭5 and it becomes the blues scale.

## The 5 box shapes (movable)
> [!info] Reading the diagrams
> High **e** string on top, low **E** on the bottom (like tab). `‖` is the nut; the column to its left is open strings. **R** = root. Shapes in *Positions* are movable: slide the whole pattern to change key.

Shown in A minor — slide them to change key.
